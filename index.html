HOW IT WORKS

One Para at a time. One word at a time.

☷

Pick Urdu, English or Urdu + English. Urdu decks are available now; the other collections are coming soon, Insha Allah.

↻

QUICK GUIDE

Choose. Practice. Remember.

Step 1: Select your language and Para from the 30 numbered cards. Urdu is available now; English and bilingual decks are being prepared.

Step 2: Once the vocabulary loads, choose a question range or a 10-question group. Generate Flashcards to begin.

Step 3: Select MCQ or Classic, then Learning or Test Mode. Flip with Space, move between questions, shuffle or restart. In Test Mode, finish to view your results.

Step 4: Download your deck in one of the original available formats. Use Change Para to switch collections.

PRIVACY

Learn with simplicity.

No login or account is required. Public Quran vocabulary DOCX resources are downloaded from this website to your browser when you select a Para. Parsing and flashcard study take place on your device; this version does not send your answers to a DeTLeng account or study-results database. Study progress during the current session may reset when you close or refresh the page.

Like other websites, the hosting platform and external links may process standard request information. Downloaded dictionary PDFs and vocabulary documents are public educational resources, not private uploads.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A personal journey toward understanding.

For many years, Muhammad Naveed Ishaque preserved a 39-page Urdu Quran dictionary from Understand Al-Quran Academy and thought about how Quranic vocabulary could be learned more easily and remembered longer. On revisiting the Academy’s website he discovered the expanded 50-page edition, which became the reference for preparing this project’s current vocabulary material.

Inspired by that journey, he brought the prepared Para vocabulary documents together with the DeTLeng Flashcard learning experience. This independent educational project hopes to make Quranic word learning easier, more approachable and useful to learners around the world.

Dedicated to beloved parents

This humble effort is dedicated to his beloved parents. May Allah Almighty grant them forgiveness, His mercy, peace and the highest ranks in Jannatul Firdaus. May this work become beneficial knowledge and Sadaqah Jariyah for them and for humanity. Ameen, Ya Rabbal Aalameen.

With gratitude

We acknowledge Understand Al-Quran Academy and the Quran dictionary authors Dr. Abdul Aziz Abdul Rahim and Muhammad Mustafa Shareef. This independent DeTLeng project is not an official Academy website. Original vocabulary content has not been rewritten by this website.
